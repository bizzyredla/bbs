The problem

Every partner leader gets asked the same three questions.

Most teams answer them with a spreadsheet, a CRM field nobody fills in, and a portal partners stopped logging into. Covalent answers them from data.

Asked by the CFO

“Which partners actually drive revenue?”

A single “partner source” field gives all the credit to whoever registered first. The SI that ran the technical win and the reseller that closed procurement get nothing.

Partner Ledger records every touch on every deal and splits credit by the model you choose.

Asked by the CRO

“Why did most of our partners go quiet?”

Partners sign up with enthusiasm, never register a deal, and nobody notices until the annual review. By then the relationship is gone.

Partner Pulse scores every partner weekly and starts a playbook when activity drops.

Asked by your partners

“When do I get paid, and how much?”

Commission math lives in a finance spreadsheet. Partners wait weeks, dispute the number, and send their next deal to a vendor that pays on time.

Payout Autopilot calculates statements from closed deals and shows partners their earnings live.

One platform, seven stages

Built for the whole life of a partnership, not only deal registration.

Each stage hands its data to the next. The overlap that helped you recruit a partner tells your AE who to call. The touches logged while co-selling become the payout statement.

Questions

Common questions

Does Covalent replace HubSpot or Salesforce?

No. Your CRM stays the system of record for deals and customers. Covalent becomes the system of record for partners and partner activity, and syncs both ways so AEs see partner context on every opportunity without leaving the CRM.

Do partners have to log into a portal?

Only if they want to. Partners can register deals, ask for collateral and check payouts from Slack, Microsoft Teams, email, or their own CRM. The portal is there for partners who prefer it.

How is partner influence calculated?

The Partner Ledger collects touches from your CRM, calendar, call recorder and messaging tools, and partners can log their own with proof. You choose an attribution model (first touch, weighted, even split or custom), and every report and payout uses it.

How does account mapping stay private?

Each side shares hashed company domains, and Covalent only reveals matches both partners agreed to share. You choose which segments are visible, such as open opportunities only, and can revoke access at any time.

Who is Covalent built for?

B2B SaaS companies from Series A to public, with anywhere from 10 to a few thousand partners: resellers, referral partners, agencies, systems integrators, technology partners and marketplaces.

How long does setup take?

The target is a working program in under a week: connect your CRM, import partners from a spreadsheet, choose a program template, and invite partners into Slack Connect or the portal.
