Questions

Questions Worth Asking Your Insperity Rep

Fair questions any client can ask. The answers make any comparison more accurate.

  1. What SUI rate are you applying in each state, and is it under your account or ours?
  2. What does the Co-Employment Risk allocation fund?
  3. Can you itemize Missed Medical and Missed Admin by employee for the last 3 months?
  4. Is there a charge for each bonus or off-cycle check?
  5. What is our workers' comp rate by class code, separate from EPLI?
  6. What is the renewal increase by line: benefits, workers' comp, and admin?
